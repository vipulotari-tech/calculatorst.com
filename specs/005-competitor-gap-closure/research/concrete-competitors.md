## 2. CalculatorSoup

- Round Slab
- Wall
- Footer

**Total: 9 shapes** (most comprehensive of all competitors)

### Input Options and Fields
**Common fields per mode:**
- Depth, Length, Width, Diameter, Height (varies by shape)
- Unit selection per dimension: ft, in, cm, m
- Quantity field (multiplies volume for repeating items like tubes)

**Optional Cost Calculation:**
- Currency selector: $, Euro, Pound, Rupee, Yuan, Yen, Rand, generic
- Price per cubic foot, cubic yard, or cubic meter

### Unit Systems Supported
- **Imperial:** feet, inches
- **Metric:** centimeters, meters
- **Output:** Cubic Yards, Cubic Feet, Cubic Meters

### Results Provided
- Volume in cubic yards, cubic feet, and cubic meters
- Optional total cost based on entered unit price
- Reference data:
  - "About 45 bags (80-lb), 60 bags (60-lb) or 90 bags (40-lb)"
  - Weight: "Roughly 3,700-4,100 lb (about 2 tons)"
  - Overage guidance: "Add 5-10% for waste, spillage, uneven subgrade and over-excavation"

### Diagram Quality and Type
Schematic illustrations accompany each formula. Each mode shows a technical illustration with labeled dimensional arrows:
- Square slab, round slab, wall, footer, square column, round column
- Tube (outer/inner diameter, height)
- Steps (platform depth, rise, run, width)
- Curb & gutter (curb depth/height, flag thickness, gutter width, length)

### Formula Transparency
Formulas displayed beneath each calculator:

**Square/Rectangular:**
- Volume (ft³) = Width x Length x Depth (or Height)
- yd³ = ft³ / 27
- m³ = ft³ x 0.0283

**Round forms:**
- Area (ft²) = Pi x (Diameter / 2)²
- Volume (ft³) = Depth x Area

**Tube (hollow):**
- Volume = Height x (Outer Area - Inner Area)

**Steps:** Section-based approach summing platform volume plus individual step volumes.

**Curb & Gutter:** Sums curb section plus gutter section.

Pi = 3.14

### Worked Examples
**"Filling Round Concrete Tubes" example:**
- 50 tubes, 4 ft high, 10 in diameter
- Per tube: 0.08 cubic yards
- Total: 4 cubic yards
- Recommends adding 10-15% for spillage/loss

### Thickness Guide Table
Recommended thicknesses for common projects:
- Sidewalk/walkway: 4 in
- Patio/terrace: 4 in
- Driveway (cars): 4-5 in
- Garage slab: 4-5 in
- Hot-tub pad: 6 in
- Shed pad: 4 in
- Stair treads: 3-4 in
- Footings: 8-12 in deep (varies)

### Application Categories
Three grouped lists:
- **Residential/Outdoor:** patios, sidewalks, driveways, garage/shed pads, hot-tub pads, stairs, fence/deck posts, raised beds, retaining walls
- **Foundations/Structural:** footings, isolated pads, stem walls, ramps, monolithic slabs
- **Commercial/Utility:** warehouse slabs, dock aprons, equipment pads, drainage channels, curbs/gutters

### Estimation Tips
- Break irregular shapes into rectangles/circles/trapezoids
- Sloped slabs: average thickness = (min + max) / 2
- Add 5-10% overage for waste/spillage
- Keep units consistent
- Reinforcement doesn't affect volume

### FAQ Content
7 questions:
1. How many bags per cubic yard? (~45 x 80-lb, 60 x 60-lb, 90 x 40-lb)
2. Weight per yd³ (3,700-4,100 lbs)
3. Extra concrete to order (5-10%)
4. Cure times (walk 24-48 hrs, drive 7 days, full strength 28 days)
5. Ideal pouring weather (50-70F)
6. Irregular shapes/slopes approach
7. Bagged vs. ready-mix guidance

## Key Observations

### Modes Coverage
- **CalculatorSoup** has the broadest mode coverage with 9 shapes including Tube/hollow.
- **Inch Calculator** and **Calculator.net** both have 7 modes but split unique features: Inch Calculator has Bell Footing; Calculator.net has Monolithic Slab.
- **Tube/hollow cylinder** is only supported by CalculatorSoup among the confirmed competitors.

### Formula Transparency
All three confirmed competitors display formulas. All three use similar approach: calculate in cubic feet first, then convert. Pi value varies (3.14 vs 3.14159265359).

### FAQ Depth
- CalculatorSoup has the deepest FAQ (7 items) including cure times, weather guidance, and bagged vs. ready-mix.
- Calculator.net has 5 items focused on coverage, truck capacity, bag counts, mix ratio, and weight.
- Inch Calculator has 6 items overlapping with CalculatorSoup.

### Worked Examples
Only CalculatorSoup includes a worked numerical example (filling 50 round tubes). No other competitor provides this.

### Thickness Guidance
Only CalculatorSoup provides a recommended thickness table for common project types.

### Accessibility
All confirmed competitors have skip-to-content links. Calculator.net and Inch Calculator offer dark mode. Only Inch Calculator has author citations and references.

### Mobile Strategy
- Inch Calculator promotes dedicated mobile apps (App Store / Google Play).
- Calculator.net also mentions dedicated iOS/Android apps.
- CalculatorSoup focuses on responsive web + embeddable widget.

### Unit System Completeness
- **Inch Calculator** supports the widest unit range: in, ft, yd, cm, mm (6 options).
- **CalculatorSoup** and **Calculator.net** both support 4 unit options (ft, in, cm, m; plus yd for Calculator.net).
